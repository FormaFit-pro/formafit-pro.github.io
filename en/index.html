FAQ

Is it really free?

Yes. Building programs, logging your workouts and tracking your progress is free, with no ads. Budi Pro is only for coaches with more than 3 clients.

Do I need a coach?

No. Build your own program or start from a template. And if you ever get a coach, connect with their code and get their program.

Do I need an account?

No. Open the app and train. Only coaches create an account to connect with their clients.

What about my data?

It stays on your phone. No analytics, no tracking, no ads. Only if you connect with a coach do they see the workouts from their program. More details.

Does it work on iPhone and Android?

Yes, both.
