Everything you do in a spreadsheet, in the app.

Build

Build programs with their sessions and exercises your way: sets, rep ranges, rest and notes. Add any exercise you can’t find.

Periodize

Split the program into stages: accumulation, intensification, deload… Each with its own sessions, exercises and length. The program moves on by itself, or you lock stages until you’ve reviewed them.

Reuse

Save programs and sessions as templates to assign to your clients anytime, or copy another client’s program and tweak it.

Rule-based progression

Set each exercise’s rule once and Budi suggests the weight for the next session. Your client sees it in their workout: they know what to lift without asking you.

  • What goes up: weight, reps or time, or weight by effort from the estimated 1RM
  • When it goes up: on every set or on most, with a brake if the RPE runs too high
  • What happens on a miss: hold the weight or drop 10 %, your call

FAQ

Do my clients have to pay?

No. Budi is free for them. Only you need Pro, and only if you have more than 3 clients.

What if a client doesn’t want to install the app?

Send them the session on WhatsApp from Budi, they send it back filled in and you paste their text: it goes into their history. Or, if they train with you, log their sets yourself.

How does Budi decide how much weight to add?

With the progression rule you choose for each exercise: double progression, effort-based from the estimated 1RM, with an RPE brake… Budi suggests the weight and you can always change it.

What’s in the free plan?

3 clients (1 connected through their app and 2 you log yourself or via WhatsApp), 1 program template and 1 session template. Plus your own training, no limits: programs, logging and progress. No time limit.

Can I use it on a computer?

Right now everything happens on your phone. We’re working on letting you see your clients on a computer too.

Do exercises have videos or images?

Not yet. We’re working on a professional exercise viewer.

How do I cancel Pro?

The annual subscription is managed in the App Store or Google Play, and you can cancel it there anytime. The one-time payment never expires.
