Your data

Where is my data stored?

On your phone. It only leaves your phone in three cases:

  • If you turn on the Google Drive backup. The backup is saved in your Drive.
  • If you connect with a coach. They see the sessions explained in “What does my coach see?”.
  • If you are a coach and connect your clients. Your programs go up to the server so they reach them.

The full details are in the privacy policy.

How do I move my data to another phone?

There are two ways. Both move your programs, your history and your settings.

With a file:

  1. On the old phone: Menu → DATA → Export → “Export backup”. This creates a .fitdata file.
  2. Send that file to the new phone (by email, WhatsApp or your cloud).
  3. On the new phone: Menu → DATA → “Import file” and choose the file.

With Google Drive:

  1. On the old phone: Menu → CONNECTIONS → “Google Drive backup” → turn it on.
  2. On the new phone: open the same screen and connect the same Google account.
  3. In the “Backups” tab, tap the backup you want to restore.

Important: if you have neither the file nor the Drive backup, your data is lost when you uninstall the app or lose your phone, and it cannot be recovered.

How do I delete my account?

Menu → ACCOUNT → “Delete my account”.

Your account and everything stored in it are deleted from the server. This cannot be undone.

  • If you are a coach, your clients stop being connected to you.
  • If you are a client, you stop being connected to your coach, and the history of yours that they had is deleted.

What is on your phone (history, programs and exercises) is not deleted. You don’t lose Pro either, because the purchase belongs to your Apple or Google Play account.

If you never created an account, there is nothing to delete: your data is only on your phone. To erase it, uninstall the app.

Purchases and Pro

I paid for Pro and the app is still on the free plan. What do I do?
  1. Check that the phone uses the same Apple or Google Play account you paid with.
  2. Open Menu → ACCOUNT → Plan.
  3. Tap “Restore purchase”.

If it still doesn’t show up, email us with the receipt Apple or Google sent you.

I changed phones. How do I get Pro back?

Same system (Android to Android, or iPhone to iPhone): sign in to the store with the same account you paid with and tap “Restore purchase” (Menu → ACCOUNT → Plan).

Different system (Android to iPhone, or the other way round): “Restore purchase” doesn’t work, because it is a different store. Pro comes back when you sign in with the account you saved when you bought it:

  1. Open the Clients tab. The first time, the “Client management” window opens by itself. If it doesn’t, tap the cloud icon at the top right.
  2. Sign in with the same account you saved: “With your Google account”, or “Already have a code? Recover account” and type your 12-character code.
  3. Pro turns on by itself within a few seconds. You don’t need to tap “Restore purchase”.

If you didn’t save any account when you bought it, Pro can only be recovered on the same system. Email us with the purchase receipt.

How do I cancel my subscription?

You cancel it in the store, not in the app. Uninstalling the app or deleting your account does not cancel it.

  • iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Forma Fit → Cancel Subscription. Or use this link.
  • Android: Google Play → your profile picture → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Forma Fit → Cancel. Or use this link.

You keep Pro until the last day of the year you already paid for. After that, the app moves to the free plan.

What is the difference between the yearly plan and the one-time purchase?

Both give you exactly the same. Only the way you pay changes:

  • Yearly: you pay once a year. It renews automatically every year until you cancel it.
  • One-time purchase: you pay once and it is yours forever. There are no renewals.
What happens to my clients if I no longer have Pro?

Nothing is deleted. You move to the free plan, which allows:

  • 3 clients. Of those, only 1 can be connected to the app.
  • 1 program template and 1 session template.

If you have more clients, the app asks you to choose which ones you keep. The rest are frozen:

  • You can see their profile, their history and their program, and delete them.
  • You can’t edit their program, log sessions for them, send them programs or receive their sessions.

Your connected clients keep training as usual and are not notified.

The templates you already have stay and you can use them. What you can’t do is create more.

If you go back to Pro, everything is unlocked and you receive, all at once, the sessions your clients did in the meantime.

How do I ask for a refund?

Refunds are handled by the store where you paid. We can’t issue them.

  • iPhone: reportaproblem.apple.com
  • Android: Google Play help on refunds

Coach and clients

How do I connect with my coach?
  1. Ask your coach for their code. It is 8 characters, for example XK7M-2P4T.
  2. Open Menu → CONNECTIONS → Trainer.
  3. Tap “Connect with a code” and type it in.

When you connect, your current program is archived and your coach’s program is loaded. Your earlier history is not deleted and is not sent to them.

As a client, you don’t have to pay anything.

What does my coach see?

They see: the sessions you complete from the programs they send you, and the free sessions you do from the moment you connected, with the body weight you log.

They don’t see: the programs you created yourself, or anything you trained before you connected.

I changed phones or reinstalled the app. How do I get the connection back?

Open Menu → CONNECTIONS → Trainer and:

  • If you linked your Google or Apple account, tap “Reconnect with my account”.
  • If not, tap “Connect with a code” and type the same code. The code doesn’t expire.

If the app says the slot is already taken, ask your coach to generate a new code for you. Your history is not lost.

My sessions aren’t reaching my coach.

Each session is sent automatically when you finish it, if you have internet. If it fails, the app tries again every time you open it.

To send it now: Menu → CONNECTIONS → Trainer → “Retry sending”.

How do I disconnect from my coach?

Menu → CONNECTIONS → Trainer → “Disconnect”.

You stop sending them your sessions. Their program is archived and the one you had before comes back, if you had one. You can connect again with the same code.
