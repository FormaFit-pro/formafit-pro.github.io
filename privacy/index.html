Privacy

Last updated: 8 October 2026 · Español

In short

  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking.
  • Almost everything stays on your phone. Only what is needed to connect a coach with their clients reaches our server.
  • You can delete your account from the app at any time.

1. Who is responsible

Forma Fit (the “app”) is developed by Lucas Gómez Alonso, the controller of your data.

2. What stays on your phone

If you use the app without connecting to anyone, your programs, workouts, body weight, goals and onboarding answers are stored only on your device. We never receive them.

3. What reaches our server

Only when a coach creates an account or a client connects with their coach:

DataWhenWhy
Email addressIf the coach signs in with Google or Apple. With Apple’s “Hide My Email” we receive a relay address, not the real one. With a personal code no email is asked for.To identify your account
Coach nameWhen the coach creates their accountSo their clients know who they are
Client nameThe one the coach gives the client when inviting themSo the coach can tell their clients apart
Programs and adjustmentsWhen the coach sends them to a clientSo the client receives them
Client training historyIf the client is connected. Only sessions from that coach’s programs and free sessions after connecting: exercises, sets, loads, reps, effort, duration, notes and the body weight logged in the sessionSo the coach can see how their client trains and adjust the program
User identifierAn internal account ID. The client’s is anonymous and created when connectingTo make the connection work and link purchases to your account
PurchasesWhen you buy or restore Forma Fit ProTo know whether Pro is active

A client’s profile name, goals, onboarding answers and workouts from programs that aren’t their coach’s are not uploaded.

Payments are processed by Apple or Google. We never see your card or payment details.

4. Health data and legal basis

Workouts and body weight may be considered health data. They only leave your phone if you connect with a coach: by entering their code you explicitly consent to them seeing it (GDPR art. 9.2.a). You can withdraw this consent at any time by disconnecting or deleting your account.

The rest of the data in section 3 is processed because it is needed to provide the service you ask for: your account, the coach–client connection and your purchases (GDPR art. 6.1.b).

5. Who it is shared with

  • Your coach, if you connect with them: what section 3 describes.
  • Supabase: hosts the database and accounts.
  • RevenueCat: manages purchase status.
  • Apple and Google: sign-in and payments, under their own policies.
  • Google Drive, only if you turn on backups: the file is saved to your Drive, the app can only access files it creates itself, and we have no access to it.

Supabase and RevenueCat act as processors. Supabase stores data in the European Union. RevenueCat is in the US, and that transfer relies on the safeguards the GDPR requires (the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses).

We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

6. What we don’t do

The app has no analytics, advertising, cross-app tracking or crash reporting, and it does not access your location, contacts or photos.

7. How long data is kept

For as long as you have an account. Deleting it removes your account and profile from the server. If you are a coach, your clients’ slots are removed too. If you are a client, your history is removed from your coach’s client record. Apple, Google and RevenueCat keep purchase records according to their own legal obligations.

8. Your rights

You have the right to access, rectify, erase, object to, restrict and port your data. From the app you can export it to a .fitdata file and delete your account.

Deleting your account: in the app, Menu → ACCOUNT → “Delete my account”.

If you believe we have not handled your data properly, you can complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency (aepd.es) or your local authority.

9. Children

The app is not intended for children under 14.

10. Changes

If this policy changes, we will publish it here with the update date.
